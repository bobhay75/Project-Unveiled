OOIS Field Mapper Privacy Policy

OOIS Field Mapper is a private field documentation app for recording terrain observations, site records, photos, and evidence-focused notes.

Data collected

The app may collect precise location only when the user captures GPS or saves a site record. The app may collect photos only when the user attaches them. Notes, site names, categories, and observations are entered by the user.

How data is used

Data is used to show private map markers, organize field records, sync records across the user's devices when cloud sync is enabled, and generate evidence-focused analysis summaries.

Storage

Local records may be stored on the user's device. If Firebase sync is enabled, records and photos are stored in Firebase under the user's authenticated account.

Sharing

Records are private by default. The app does not sell user data. The user may export records manually as JSON.

Deletion

Users may delete records inside the app. Cloud records are deleted from the user's Firebase record path when cloud sync is enabled.

Safety

OOIS is for lawful documentation only. It does not encourage trespassing, digging, protected-site disturbance, or artifact removal.
