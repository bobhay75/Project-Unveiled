Why This Book Exists

Truth Is Not Afraid of Questions.

This book does not ask readers to abandon Jesus, replace God, or trade one unquestioned authority for another. It challenges readers to seek, ask, knock, test, compare, examine, and follow truth with courage.

01

Tear the Veil

Truth should open access, expose false barriers, and free people from fear-driven control.

02

Test the Shelf

An approved shelf, hidden shelf, institution, or tradition is not automatically identical to God.

03

Examine the Fruit

Wealth, platform, political power, and institutional success are not proof of righteousness.

04

Love Is the Proof

Mercy, justice, compassion, humility, freedom, faithfulness, and love reveal the character of a message.

05

History Matters

Claims must be examined within their language, authorship, culture, date, audience, and setting.

06

Questions Are Not Rebellion

Honest inquiry can expose error, deepen understanding, and strengthen what survives examination.

Research Standards

Evidence Before Certainty.

Credibility requires a visible line between established evidence, interpretation, theological belief, reasonable inference, and speculation.

Primary Sources

Original Material First

Ancient texts, manuscripts, inscriptions, records, and contemporary witnesses receive priority when available.

Context

Surrounding Evidence Preserved

Language, passage, date, authorship, audience, translation, and historical setting matter.

Disagreement

Qualified Positions Represented

Major views and their supporting evidence should be presented fairly.

Uncertainty

Unknown Is an Answer

Possible, probable, disputed, and unsupported are legitimate conclusions.

Corrections

Errors Are Not Defended

Meaningful public revisions should be identified rather than quietly hidden.

AI

Not a Historical Authority

AI may assist organization and research planning; final claims must be checked against sources.
