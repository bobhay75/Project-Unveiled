The Research Promise

Evidence Before Certainty.

Facts will be stated as facts. Interpretation will be labeled as interpretation. Uncertainty will not be disguised as proof.

Project Unveiled is built to withstand honest scrutiny. The goal is not to defend every early draft or personal theory. The goal is to identify what can be supported, correct what cannot, and make the reasoning visible.

When the evidence is incomplete, conflicting, translated differently, or dependent on later tradition, that limitation belongs in the discussion.

Claim Classification

Every Claim Has a Type

These labels prevent documented history, reasonable interpretation, theology, and speculation from being blended into one category.

Documented Fact

Directly Supported

A claim supported by identifiable primary evidence, reliable documentation, or strong agreement among qualified sources.

Historical Interpretation

Evidence-Based Reading

A conclusion drawn from evidence but still dependent on interpretation, context, assumptions, or competing scholarly explanations.

Theological Belief

Faith Claim

A spiritual or theological conviction that may be meaningful and important but is not presented as independently proven historical fact.

Open Question

Not Yet Established

A possibility, suspicion, disputed issue, or unresolved question that requires more evidence before a conclusion is justified.

Source Policy

The Evidence Ladder

Sources are not equal merely because they appear in print, online, in a documentary, or in a confident presentation.

01

Primary Evidence

Ancient manuscripts, inscriptions, archaeological records, official documents, contemporary witnesses, letters, legal records, and original-language texts.

02

Qualified Scholarly Analysis

Peer-reviewed research, academic monographs, critical editions, recognized historians, archaeologists, linguists, manuscript specialists, and subject experts.

03

Responsible Secondary Sources

Encyclopedias, university resources, museum materials, reputable historical summaries, and publications that clearly identify their sources.

04

Leads Requiring Verification

Videos, social-media posts, unsourced websites, documentaries, sermons, podcasts, personal testimony, and AI-generated summaries may provide leads but are not final authorities.

Public Corrections Log

Errors Will Be Corrected Openly.

No Published Corrections Yet

This log will record meaningful factual corrections made to publicly released Project Unveiled material. Minor spelling, formatting, or punctuation edits may be corrected without a formal entry.

  • The original claim will be identified.
  • The corrected information will be stated clearly.
  • The reason for the change will be explained.
  • Supporting sources will be identified when available.
  • Corrections will not be hidden merely to protect pride.

Transparency Disclosure

How AI Is Used

AI tools may assist with organization, comparison, outlining, editing, coding, source discovery, and research planning.

AI output is not treated as historical evidence or as an authority merely because it sounds confident. Important claims must be checked against identifiable sources before final publication.

AI May Assist With

  • Organizing research questions
  • Comparing competing explanations
  • Drafting and editing language
  • Building the public website
  • Locating possible sources for review
  • Identifying claims requiring verification
Truth Can Withstand Examination

Bring the Evidence.

Serious criticism, stronger sources, factual corrections, competing interpretations, and documented challenges are welcome when offered honestly.
