The Way Begins

30–100 AD

The first followers of Jesus live and teach in His way. Oral teachings, letters, and eyewitness testimony spread across the nations.

THE STORY

Jesus taught the Kingdom of God. He called people to repent, believe, and live by love. His followers—called the Way—shared meals, prayed, healed the sick, and cared for the poor. There was no empire behind them and no state-backed centralized institution. The message spread from Jerusalem to Antioch, Asia Minor, Rome, and beyond.

“They turned the world upside down…” — Acts 17:6

KEY POINTS

  • Oral teachings and eyewitness accounts
  • Letters and messages to churches
  • Persecution but rapid growth
  • The Way was a life, not a religion
