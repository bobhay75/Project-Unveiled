Reader Reviews & Scholarly Review.

Project Unveiled invites response, criticism, and serious examination. Reader reactions and expert reviews belong in public view when permission and attribution are clear—but they are not substitutes for evidence.

Two Different Lanes

Response is not evidence.

A reader may describe what the book did for them. A qualified reviewer may challenge its reasoning or historical treatment. Neither turns an unsupported historical claim into a fact.

Reader Reviews

What readers experienced

Reader reviews may address clarity, impact, questions raised, spiritual response, disagreement, usefulness, or the experience of reading the full work.

  • Permission required before publication.
  • Attribution must be clear.
  • Material relationships should be disclosed.
  • Edits for length or clarity will be identified when material.

Scholarly / Expert Review

What qualified critics conclude

Historians, theologians, clergy, religious-studies scholars, manuscript specialists, educators, and other relevant experts are invited to review claims, method, sourcing, interpretation, and argument.

  • Credentials or relevant expertise must be verifiable.
  • Critical reviews are welcome, not filtered out for disagreement.
  • Conflicts of interest should be disclosed.
  • Historical corrections are routed to the Research & Corrections log.

Publication Standard

How a review earns a place here.

01 · Identity

Know who said it

A public name, approved abbreviated attribution, or clearly described identity must be supplied. Expert status is never implied from an anonymous quote.

02 · Permission

Permission is explicit

Submitting feedback privately does not automatically authorize public publication. Permission must be clear.

03 · Separation

Reviews stay reviews

Endorsement, criticism, testimony, factual correction, and historical evidence are labeled as different things rather than blended together.

“If Project Unveiled asks readers to test inherited claims, the project has to accept the same test.”Project Unveiled review standard
Useful Submission

Include enough context to verify and publish responsibly

  • Your name and preferred public attribution.
  • Whether you read the whole book or specific chapters.
  • The exact review text you authorize for publication.
  • Any relationship to Robert J. Hayes or the project.
  • For scholarly review: relevant degree, role, publication, ministry, or subject expertise.
  • For factual challenges: the specific claim plus the strongest source you have.

Corrections remain governed by the public Research & Corrections process and may be published even when they disagree with the book.
