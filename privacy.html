Privacy

Project Unveiled uses limited first-party readership analytics. The purpose is to understand which pages are being read, which campaigns bring visitors, whether readers move between chapters, and how often support links are clicked.

What is recorded

Page paths, anonymous browser-tab session identifiers, campaign labels such as utm_source, referring website names, chapter-navigation activity, share-button clicks, support-page clicks, and PayPal-link clicks.

What is not recorded

No IP address is stored. No advertising profile is created. No cross-site tracking cookie is used. The analytics do not reveal whether a PayPal payment was completed or disclose payment information.

Your browser controls

The tracker respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. Its anonymous session identifier is stored only in the current browser tab and ends when that tab session ends.

Purpose and retention

The information is used to improve Project Unveiled, understand readership, and evaluate outreach. Analytics files are stored privately outside the public website directory and may be deleted when no longer useful.

Reader signup information

When you join the Project Unveiled reader list, we collect your first name, email address, signup time, source page, campaign tags, and privacy-safe abuse-prevention hashes. We use this information to send Project Unveiled updates, understand which campaigns bring readers, protect the form from abuse, and honor unsubscribe requests.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.

You may unsubscribe through any Project Unveiled email.
