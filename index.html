Mission Statement

Research Standards

Evidence Before Certainty.

The credibility of Project Unveiled depends on making the line between evidence, interpretation, belief, inference, and speculation visible.

Facts

Documented as Facts

Historical claims are tied to identifiable sources and presented in context.

Interpretation

Labeled Clearly

Theological positions and reasonable inferences are not presented as unquestionable proof.

Uncertainty

Admitted Openly

Disputed, unknown, probable, possible, and unsupported remain valid conclusions.

Sources

Primary Material First

Ancient texts, official records, manuscripts, and identifiable historical documents receive priority.

Disagreement

Major Positions Represented

Qualified disagreement belongs on the page rather than being quietly removed.

Corrections

Errors Corrected Openly

Meaningful revisions should be identified rather than hidden or defended.
