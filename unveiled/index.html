The path through the veil

Seven days. Seven truths worth sitting with.

This is not a rushed argument or a list of conclusions you must accept. Each day gives you a truth to examine, Scripture to read in context, and a question to carry honestly.

  1. Day 1

    The truth does not need your belief.

    Belief can receive truth or resist it, but belief does not manufacture what is true.

  2. Day 2

    You do not believe yourself into being a child.

    A child’s origin comes before the child’s understanding. What changes when identity begins with the Father?

  3. Day 3

    Life is in the breath.

    Before labels, institutions, and arguments, there is the breath of life—and the One from whom it comes.

  4. Day 4

    The Kingdom is nearer than you were told.

    Jesus did not point only to a distant destination. He spoke of a Kingdom already in your midst.

  5. Day 5

    Wearing Christianity is not the same as following Christ.

    Titles and costumes are easy to display. Following the Way becomes visible in how we live and love.

  6. Day 6

    Judge the tree by its fruit.

    Power, popularity, and performance are not the test Jesus gave. Fruit is.

  7. Day 7

    Ask. Seek. Knock. Tear the veil.

    The final day is not an ending. It is permission to keep seeking with courage, humility, and love.

“Truth is not afraid of questions.”
