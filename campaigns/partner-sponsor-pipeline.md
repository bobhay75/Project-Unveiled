# Bobsome1 Partner + Sponsor Pipeline

Updated: September 13, 2026

## First-contact rule

Lead with specific value or a tightly bounded collaboration idea. Do not send prices in an unsolicited first contact. Do not promise favorable coverage, undisclosed placement, exclusivity, audience size, sales, or outcomes that are not verified.

## Reusable first-contact draft

**Subject:** A small, testable partnership idea for [organization]

I’m Robert J. Hayes, the builder behind Bobsome1.com in the Branson/Table Rock area. I combine construction-field experience with websites, media systems, practical IT and evidence-first research.

I noticed [one specific, verified point about their program or audience]. I have a small collaboration idea: [one-sentence test] that would give your audience [specific value] while giving us a real-world result we can document honestly.

I do not sell guaranteed praise or hide material support. If the fit is real, I would propose a bounded test, visible disclosure and a simple success measure before either side commits to anything larger.

Would it be useful if I sent the one-page concept?

Robert J. Hayes
https://bobsome1.com/store/#partners

## Sequence

1. Personalize and send only the first two local contacts first.
2. Apply to one technology creator program with a precise test concept.
3. Add affiliate links only after approval, with disclosure and page-level relevance.
4. Track replies, introductions, supplied value and revenue separately.
5. Publish no sponsor logo or relationship until terms and permission are documented.
