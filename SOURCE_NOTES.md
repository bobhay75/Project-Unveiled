## Guardrails Applied

- Primary does not mean neutral; it means the document can be examined directly.
- The source transmission of Constantine's anti-Arius order is disclosed.
- The theory linking burial of the Nag Hammadi codices to Athanasius's 367 letter is labeled as a hypothesis.
- Nicaea's surviving documents are distinguished from later canon lists.
- Different Christian Old Testament collections are acknowledged rather than flattened into one shelf.
