              Privacy / deliberate exits

              Your draft case begins on this device.

              Typing, mapping, attaching, fingerprinting, and saving a case happen in this browser. Saving uses this browser profile's local storage; another person using the same profile may see it.

              No external search runs until you press “Run Source Sweep.” Starting it sends the claim-derived query text to Crossref, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Internet Archive, and the Federal Register. Their privacy and logging practices apply. Trust-Worthy records returned metadata and failures locally; it does not send your saved docket or manually attached evidence to those indexes.

              Local receipts are not a trust authority. Browser storage can be edited, cleared, or forged by a user, extension, or local software. On reopen, provider identities and queries are rebuilt from the fixed plan and prior execution is labeled unverified. The app preserves the exact save-time canonical JSON and checks its stored fingerprint when possible, but neither one is an external timestamp or proof of review.

              Hosting boundary: This public app is delivered through ChatGPT Sites and network/security providers. They may process ordinary request metadata, IP addresses, security cookies, and access logs under their own terms; Trust-Worthy does not receive a private analytics dashboard from this browser-local case file.

Copy actions use your clipboard. Source links, email, Facebook, and later payment instructions leave this site and are handled by those services.
