# Trust-Worthy Holiday Origins Series

## Editorial question

**Where did America’s major holidays and holiday customs actually come from?**

Each Truth Trial should distinguish:

- the earliest documented origin of the observance or custom;
- older religious, civic, seasonal, folk, or cultural practices that may have influenced it;
- when and how the observance entered American life;
- government action, church practice, immigration, commerce, advertising, and popular culture that reshaped it;
- later stories or claims about the holiday that are repeated more confidently than the evidence supports;
- what is proven, strongly indicated, inferred, disputed, and unknown.

Do not assume that a modern holiday is either purely religious, purely pagan, purely commercial, or unchanged from an ancient practice. Trace each element separately.
