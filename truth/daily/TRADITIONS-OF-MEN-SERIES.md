# Trust-Worthy: Origins of the Traditions of Men

## Core investigation

**Which beliefs, rules, rituals, titles, customs, and institutions commonly presented as Christian can be traced directly to Jesus and the earliest followers, and which developed later through theology, church government, empire, culture, translation, economics, or inherited tradition?**

The phrase “traditions of men” is treated here as an investigation question, not a predetermined verdict. Each Trial must trace the evidence before classifying a practice.

## Standard method for every tradition

1. What exactly is the modern belief or practice?
2. Is it explicitly taught by Jesus in the surviving Gospel record?
3. Is it present in the earliest Christian writings and communities?
4. What is the earliest securely dated evidence for it?
5. How did the teaching or practice change over time?
6. Who formalized, enforced, standardized, or popularized it?
7. What role did empire, politics, money, social control, philosophy, translation, or surrounding culture play, if any?
8. What are the strongest arguments for continuity with the earliest faith?
9. What are the strongest arguments that it is a later development?
10. What is proven, strongly indicated, reasonably inferred, disputed, and unknown?
11. What fruit has the teaching or practice historically produced?

## Governing rule

Do not label something “pagan,” “biblical,” “Roman,” “apostolic,” or “a tradition of men” merely because a later writer says so. Trace the earliest evidence, chronology, continuity, discontinuity, and competing explanations.
