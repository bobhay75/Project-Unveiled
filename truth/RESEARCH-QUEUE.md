# Trust-Worthy Research Queue

A question enters private storage as a structured research record.

## Required stages

1. `queued` — recorded privately; no answer claimed.
2. `define` — question separated into a testable claim and assumptions.
3. `trace` — source ledger records primary and earliest accessible evidence.
4. `counterevidence` — strongest credible contrary material is recorded.
5. `draft` — answer separates evidence, inference, interpretation, and uncertainty.
6. `human-review` — publication and claims receive a human check.
7. `published` or `declined` — public status with a revision path.

## Record integrity

- Private identity and contact data never enter public cases.
- Every generated answer must retain a source ledger and uncertainty list.
- A public case is not created automatically by a submission.
- No model result may be labeled “investigated” until sources and counterevidence are present.
