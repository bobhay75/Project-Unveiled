# Trust-Worthy Personal Book System v0.1

## Working Product Name

For a member named Bob, the generated work is titled **The First Book of Bob**.

For other members the title is generated from their chosen public display name: **The First Book of [Name]**. Later major editions may become **The Second Book of [Name]**, etc.

## What the Book Is

The personal book is a living, versioned record of beliefs a member has been willing to put under evidence, challenge, independent Trust-Worthy research, and revision.

It is **not Scripture**, is not represented as divinely inspired, and does not replace the Bible or any other sacred text. It is the member's documented intellectual/theological record: what they believe, why they believe it, what challenged it, what survived, and where they changed their mind.

## Opening Declaration

Suggested opening language:

> This book does not claim to be Scripture. It is not a record of what I was told to believe. It is a record of what I believed strongly enough to challenge—and what remained after the challenge.
>
> Some beliefs survived. Some changed. Some were destroyed. Some questions remain unanswered.
>
> I reserve the right to be wrong. Truth does not.

## Chapter Sources

A chapter may be generated from:

- a completed Truth Trial tied to the member's Theology Profile;
- a completed Theology Battle;
- a successful challenge that materially revised a finding;
- a member's documented revision of their own belief after superior evidence;
- a substantial independent investigation accepted into the member's public profile.

A chapter must never be awarded merely because a member paid for a product or battle.

## Chapter Structure

Each chapter should preserve:

1. **The Question** — the proposition investigated.
2. **What I Believed** — the member's position entering the investigation.
3. **The Contradiction** — the strongest normalized `not-P` proposition.
4. **Why I Believed It** — the member's initial argument and evidence.
5. **The Challenge** — strongest opposing argument/evidence.
6. **Trust-Worthy Investigation** — independently researched evidence, provenance, chronology, source lineage, context, language, power/incentive analysis, logic, and alternative explanations.
7. **What Survived** — evidence that remained strong after adversarial testing.
8. **What Failed** — claims or sources that did not survive scrutiny.
9. **The Finding** — current epistemic assessment, clearly separated from truth itself.
10. **What I Believe Now** — the member's current position after the investigation.
11. **Revision Ledger** — prior versions and why they changed.
12. **Challenge Record** — meaningful public challenges and outcomes.
13. **Sources** — auditable citations and source lineage.
14. **Reopen This Question** — QR/link target to the living public case when printed.

## Edition Rules

### Living Digital Edition

The member's digital book evolves with their Theology Profile and case history. It must display the edition/version and last-updated date.

### First Physical Edition

A physical **First Book of [Name]** may be generated after a meaningful threshold of completed, audited material. The threshold should measure substance rather than victories—for example a minimum number of completed investigations and enough content for a useful volume.

### Later Editions

A later edition should not erase earlier positions. **The Second Book of [Name]** can document the member's later understanding while preserving links to the First Book and its revision history.

## Reward Philosophy

The system rewards demonstrated seeking, research, correction, and intellectual courage—not doctrinal conformity.

High-value rewards may include:

- professionally typeset digital edition;
- premium hardcover edition;
- numbered limited edition;
- leather-bound archival edition;
- engraved compass or Project Unveiled artifact;
- championship plaque;
- professionally produced documentary/video of a major investigation;
- featured publication on Bobsome1.com;
- inclusion in an annual Trust-Worthy anthology.

A particularly important achievement is changing one's own position when better evidence warrants it. Suggested distinction: **The Veil Lifted**.

## Monetization Boundary

Members may pay for services around an investigation: research compute, scheduled live battle rooms, transcription, publishing, archival storage, premium visualization, or physical production.

Payment must never purchase a finding, evidentiary score, `Unveiled` status, successful challenge, or chapter conclusion.

Prize/tournament mechanics involving entry fees and valuable awards require jurisdiction-specific legal review before launch. The initial product should prefer sponsor-funded rewards, guaranteed member products, achievement-based recognition, and clearly disclosed purchase terms rather than winner-take-all pooled entry fees.

## Strategic Value

The personal book makes the member's Theology Profile tangible. Instead of a static denomination label, the member develops a lifelong auditable record of what they have examined.

The product loop becomes:

`Belief -> Challenge -> Investigation -> Battle/Trial -> Revision -> Profile -> Book Chapter -> New Challenge`

The physical book is therefore not merely merchandise. It is a snapshot of a living evidence record whose underlying cases remain challengeable.
